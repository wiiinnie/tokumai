# Purchase consent — wording version `2026-09-07`

This file is the record of **what a buyer actually read** when they agreed. `Inv.consent_version`
in `state.db` stores the string above and nothing else; the sentences live here. A rewording
gets a **new file and a new version string** — never an edit to this one, or an invoice would
point at a text its buyer never saw.

## Where it is shown

In the buy sheet (`public/index.html`, `renderContinue()`), directly above the order button,
after the amount and the payment method are chosen and before any invoice exists. Two separate
checkboxes, neither pre-ticked, not combinable. The button is inert until both are ticked.

Not shown for the **invite tile**: the faucet pays it, the buyer pays nothing, so it is not the
paid contract § 312j BGB is about.

## The text, verbatim

Intro line above the boxes:

> Sold under our **Terms**. Please read the **right of withdrawal** and the **privacy notice**
> first.

Checkbox 1 — the express request (§ 356 (5) BGB):

> I ask you to **start delivering the credit immediately**, before the 14-day withdrawal period
> ends.

Checkbox 2 — the acknowledgement:

> I understand that I **lose my right of withdrawal** once the credit has been delivered in
> full.

Order button, when both are ticked:

> Buy now — $10 by card ↗

with the line beneath it:

> binding order · payment due on confirmation

## The three gates

1. **App** — the button carries no click handler until both boxes are ticked.
2. **`invoice` command** (`src-tauri/src/lib.rs`) — refuses to send a non-invite create whose
   consent is missing or incomplete.
3. **Server** (`pay.rs::begin_create`) — records the version with the invoice, and refuses the
   create outright when `REQUIRE_CONSENT=1`.

Gate 3 is **off by default on purpose**. Apps older than this change send no consent at all;
turning it on before `MIN_APP` retires them would stop every installed build from buying.
Turn both on together.

## Still owed

The wording above is mine, not a lawyer's. It follows the shape § 356 (5) requires — express
request plus acknowledgement, separate and unticked — but the exact sentences should be
confirmed before the app is offered beyond the tester group. A correction costs nothing but a
new version file: nothing in the code hardcodes this text except the one place it is rendered.
