Where tokumai is different

Privacy that doesn't depend on our good behaviour

  • No promise required. A relay's privacy depends on the company running it doing what it says, today and in the future. tokumai's privacy doesn't depend on our good behaviour: our servers have no way of learning where a request came from.
  • No VPN needed. A relay server sees your real IP address unless you hide it yourself. With tokumai, hiding it is built in.
  • No subscription, no store account. You buy a small amount of credit once and use it whenever you like. There is no monthly bill and no account that ties your purchase to your questions.
  • What you didn't mean to share stays home. Before a prompt leaves your device, tokumai checks it for personal data — names, addresses, IDs, also inside attached images and documents — and offers to redact it. Attached files lose their metadata on the way out: camera model, GPS position, author name, timestamps. Nothing about that check ever leaves the device.
  • Your questions never add up to a profile. Without an identity, an IP address or a traceable payment, there is nothing to build a picture of you from. Ask about your health, your finances or your relationship, and it stays a question, not a data point about you.

Frequently asked questions
Is Duck.ai really private?

Duck.ai removes identifying information before passing your prompt to the AI provider, and the providers are not allowed to train on your chats. The weak point is DuckDuckGo's own relay: it sees your IP address and your prompt together and promises not to keep them. Whether that's enough depends on how much you trust one company.

Is tokumai a Duck.ai alternative?

Yes. Both give you private access to leading AI models. tokumai is for people who want privacy that doesn't depend on trusting the provider.

Do I need a VPN with tokumai?

No. Every request already travels through the Nym mixnet, which hides where it came from.

Can tokumai see my prompts?

Our server passes your prompt on to the AI model, so it handles the text, but it never learns who sent it. Nothing you ask is stored on our servers; your conversation history lives only on your device.

Why pay for tokumai when Duck.ai is free?

Because a free service has to be paid for somehow, and because tokumai's protection comes from its design rather than a policy. Most people spend a few dollars and use it for months.
