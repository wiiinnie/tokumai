Privacy

Controller: Hermes Blockchain Ventures — Matthias Winter (sole proprietorship / Einzelunternehmen), Schraudolphstraße 2A, 80799 München, Germany, hermes-stakepool@proton.me. Version 29 August 2026.

1. The short version

tokumai is built so that we learn as little as possible. There is no account, no e-mail, no sign-up. Your questions travel through the Nym mixnet, so our server never sees your IP address. Your conversation history stays on your device, encrypted. Credit is spent as blind-signed tokens that cannot be linked to the purchase that created them.

2. This website

The site is served by us. No access logs are kept (request logging is switched off on both the reverse proxy and the application), no cookies are set, and no third-party scripts or analytics are loaded. Two honest limits: the proxy's error log can briefly record the IP address of a connection that failed (for example a broken TLS handshake), and the faucet counts claim attempts per IP address for one hour to stop brute force — hashed with a random key that changes at every restart, held in memory only, never written to disk. The testnet faucet form sends the invite code and the invoice memo you type to our server, where they are stored together with the transaction id of the test payment (purpose: pay one test invoice per code; legal basis Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR; kept for the duration of the test programme).

3. The app

  • Requests to AI models reach our server through the Nym mixnet and are forwarded to the provider of the model you chose — currently Google (Gemini) or OpenAI (ChatGPT models); the app names the provider and its terms before you use a model. We do not store the content of requests or answers. The provider processes the content to generate the answer; providers used under API terms state that API input is not used to train their models.
  • Conversations: we keep none. A prompt exists on our server only in memory and only for as long as it takes to get the answer from the model; it is never written to disk and never logged. The answer is held in memory for up to ten minutes so that a reply lost on the mixnet can be sent again without charging you twice, then dropped. Attachments and generated pictures are staged in memory the same way, for minutes, and swept. Our log records counts — tokens, cost, a session's abuse strikes — never content. Your conversation history lives on your device alone.
  • Session and credit data: the server holds anonymous session keys, balances and burned token serials — needed to bill requests and prevent double spending (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR). None of it contains a name, address or IP.
  • Purchase data: a settled purchase stays linked to the account it was made from — or, for a code bought on this site, to the code it produced — for seven days. That link is what lets us trace a payment that did not arrive and judge a refund. After seven days it is deleted; what remains of the purchase is the amount, date, payment method and country, kept for bookkeeping. This link never reaches what you ask: the account a purchase belongs to and the session that carries your prompts are not connected on our side — credit passes between them as blind-signed coins. App Store purchases (iPhone) reach us as Apple's signed transaction: product, transaction number, date, storefront country. We keep a fingerprint of the transaction number (so it cannot be credited twice), the amount, date and country; the account link follows the same seven days. Apple does not learn your tokumai account, and we do not learn your Apple ID.
  • Aggregate metrics: per-day totals (number of requests, cost, distinct sessions) without identifiers.
  • Data on your device: recovery phrase, history and settings are stored locally by the app; the privacy guard's checks run on the device only.

4. Payments

Coins (NYM, Bitcoin): we see the transaction on the public chain and the invoice memo; no personal data is collected. Cards: processed by Mollie B.V., Keizersgracht 126, 1015 CW Amsterdam. Mollie collects the data needed for the payment (card details, name, e-mail if asked, IP address, browser data) as an independent controller under its own privacy policy: mollie.com/privacy. We receive from Mollie only the payment status, amount and our own random order id — never card data. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR; Mollie's retention follows its legal obligations (payment records, typically 7 years).

5. Recipients and transfers

Model providers (Google LLC and OpenAI, L.L.C. — USA, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or standard contractual clauses) receive request content only; Mollie B.V. (Netherlands) receives card payment data; our hosting provider OVHcloud (EU data centre) operates the server. No data is sold or used for advertising.

6. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR) and the right to complain to a supervisory authority (for us: Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA), Ansbach). Note that most data we hold is anonymous by design and cannot be attributed to you; for card payments, please quote the order id from the Mollie receipt.
