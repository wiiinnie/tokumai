Privacy

Controller: Hermes Blockchain Ventures — Matthias Winter (sole proprietorship / Einzelunternehmen), Schraudolphstraße 2A, 80799 München, Germany, hermes-stakepool@proton.me. Version 21 September 2026.

1. The short version

tokumai is built so that we learn as little as possible. There is no account in the usual sense: no e-mail, no sign-up, no name — only a key derived from a recovery phrase that is generated on your device and never leaves it. Your questions travel through the Nym mixnet, so our server never sees your IP address. Your conversation history stays on your device, encrypted. Every answer is paid with blind-signed coins that cannot be linked to the account or the payment they came from.

One thing a mixnet cannot hide: the entry gateway — the first node your app connects to — sees your real IP address, the way any server you connect to does. It sees neither what you send nor where it is going: the traffic is encrypted in layers and mixed with other people's on its way. You choose the entry gateway in the app and can change it at any time.

2. This website

The site is served by us. No access logs are kept (request logging is switched off on both the reverse proxy and the application), no cookies are set, and no third-party scripts or analytics are loaded. Three honest limits: we count page views, downloads and the number of visitors per day — a visitor is recognised by a fingerprint of IP address and browser name, hashed with a random key that exists only in memory and changes every day, so the stored value identifies nobody and cannot be compared across days; the proxy's error log can briefly record the IP address of a connection that failed (for example a broken TLS handshake), and the faucet counts claim attempts per IP address for one hour to stop brute force — hashed with a random key that changes at every restart, held in memory only, never written to disk. The testnet faucet form sends the invite code and the invoice memo you type to our server, where they are stored together with the transaction id of the test payment (purpose: pay one test invoice per code; legal basis Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR; kept for the duration of the test programme).

3. The app

  • Requests to AI models reach our server through the Nym mixnet and are forwarded to the provider of the model you chose — currently Google (Gemini) or OpenAI (ChatGPT models); the app names the provider and its terms before you use a model. For OpenAI models, the message you send is first checked by OpenAI's moderation service, and a message it flags is not forwarded; this check is part of the same processing by OpenAI and is not stored by us. We do not store the content of requests or answers. The provider processes the content to generate the answer; providers used under API terms state that API input is not used to train their models.
  • Conversations: we keep none. A prompt exists on our server only in memory and only for as long as it takes to get the answer from the model; it is never written to disk and never logged. The answer is held in memory for up to ten minutes so that a reply lost on the mixnet can be sent again without charging you twice, then dropped. Attachments and generated pictures are staged in memory the same way, for minutes, and swept. Our log records counts — tokens and cost — never content. Your conversation history lives on your device alone.
  • Credit data: the server holds the serial numbers of coins that have been spent — needed to refuse a coin a second time — and, per account key, the credit and allowance not yet drawn (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR). A request carries no session and no identifier, only the coins that pay for it. None of this contains a name, address or IP.
  • Plan data: a plan has to stay linked to the account it belongs to, or it could not renew. For as long as a plan runs we hold, per account key: the plan level, the reference of the subscription at Stripe or a fingerprint of Apple's transaction number, the end of the period that has been paid for, and the month's allowance. After a plan has ended its record stays, marked as ended, so that a payment that comes through late can pick it up again — it holds nothing but the items just named, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. The payment records we must keep for bookkeeping (amount, date, payment method, country) are separate and remain. Codes: the link between a code and the account that redeemed it is deleted after seven days. None of this reaches what you ask: an account and the requests paid from it are not connected on our side — credit passes between them as blind-signed coins. App Store plans (iPhone) reach us as Apple's signed transaction: product, transaction number, dates, storefront country. Apple does not learn your tokumai account, and we do not learn your Apple ID.
  • Aggregate metrics: per-day totals (number of requests, cost) without identifiers.
  • Support: a report sent from the app travels through the mixnet and carries no account key and no address; you read our answer in the app, against a secret only your device holds (we store its hash). A report sent from this website carries the e-mail address you give us, so that we can answer. We store the text, any picture you attach and our replies, to deal with the request (Art. 6 (1) (b), (f) GDPR), and they are deleted automatically twelve months after the last message in the thread.
  • Data on your device: recovery phrase, history and settings are stored locally by the app; the privacy guard's checks run on the device only.

4. Payments

Cards and PayPal: processed by Stripe Payments Europe, Limited, 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Grand Canal Dock, Dublin 2, Ireland. The checkout runs on Stripe's own page, never in our app or on this site. For a plan, Stripe keeps a customer record and the payment method so that it can charge the next period; you manage or cancel it through the link in Stripe's receipt.

Stripe collects what the payment and our bookkeeping need — your name and billing address, your e-mail address, the card or account details, your IP address and browser data — as an independent controller under its own privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy. Name and address are required because we are obliged to record where a sale was made; they are not something the service itself needs, and they never touch your tokumai account or anything you do with it.

From Stripe we receive the payment status, the amount, our own random order id and the country of the billing address. The country is the only one of those we keep, in our sales records. We never receive card data, and we do not copy your name, address or e-mail into our systems — they stay with Stripe, where the tax records are kept. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR for the payment, Art. 6 (1) (c) GDPR for the records we must keep; Stripe's retention follows its own legal obligations (payment records, typically 7 years).

6. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR) and the right to complain to a supervisory authority (for us: Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA), Ansbach). Note that most data we hold is anonymous by design and cannot be attributed to you; for card payments, please quote the order id from the Stripe receipt.
